Discover My Story

From community-based volunteer teaching in the Philippines to special education leadership and graduate teaching across borders.

A Journey of Service and Leadership

2007

Where It Began

Started volunteering as a teacher through the Iglesia ni Cristo Summer Kindergarten Program in the Philippines.

2009

Access to Education

Helped establish an Alternative Learning System (ALS) learning initiative for out-of-school youth and adults in Bagong Silang, Caloocan, through an Iglesia ni Cristo community project in partnership with New Era University. Continued teaching on weekends.

2013

First Formal School Role

Began working as a guidance counselor at a private school in Quezon City, Philippines.

2014

Teaching and Technology

Developed a school website, taught homeroom from kindergarten through high school, and supported another school branch while continuing ALS volunteer teaching.

2016

Instructional Leadership

Appointed Head Instructional Manager of ALS. Served as part-time faculty at New Era University, teaching Literacy Training Service and supporting outreach to an Aeta community in Pampanga.

2017

Recognizing Community Impact

Received Best Head Instructional Manager recognition; the ALS center was also honored as Best Learning Center.

2019

Beyond Graduation

Developed a vision to connect ALS graduates to college scholarships, internships, and employment opportunities.

2020

Building UIP Incorporated

Officially registered UIP Incorporated (Unified Internship Program) and served as Director. Expanded virtual internship support during the pandemic and developed partnerships with more than 300 universities within two years.

2022

Special Education in the United States

Began working as a Special Education Teacher and Case Manager at Chester Senior High School, South Carolina.

2023

Educational Technology and Organizational Recognition

Served as a GRIT Educational Technology Mentor. Unified Internship Program received Asia CEO Awards Circle of Excellence recognition for Microsourcing Service Excellence Company of the Year.

2024

School Leadership and Recognition

Took on Special Education Department Chair and teacher mentoring responsibilities. Received Teacher of the Month recognition at Chester Senior High School.

2025

Doctoral Milestone and District Award

Completed doctoral studies and was recognized as Chester County School District Rockstar Teacher of the Month in February.

2026

Graduate Education and Research Mentorship

Joined University of Perpetual Help System DALTA graduate teaching, supporting master’s and doctoral students in special education, research advising, and panel review.
